# Manager User Guide

Managers record end-of-day farm activity for assigned farms only.

## Daily Workflow

1. Sign in at `/login`.
2. Open the farms, blocks, and crop seasons assigned to your account.
3. Submit the daily report for each active crop season you managed that day.
4. Record labor, expenses, inventory usage, irrigation, inputs, field notes, issues, harvests, and sales where the app section allows it.
5. Confirm submitted records are visible for owner review.

## Access Rules

Managers cannot manage users and must not see unrelated farms. If a farm is missing, ask an owner/admin to update your assignments from `/settings`.

## Passwords

If your account was created with a temporary password, change it from `/settings` after signing in.
